Inventory Models Dissertation

2007 – 2008 · Graduate research, Bharathidasan University
Operations ResearchInventory Modeling Mathematical Modeling

Graduate dissertation project exploring deterministic inventory models, including ABC analysis, generalized inventory models, and single- and multi-item deterministic models. The work focused on minimizing inventory costs, evaluating the impact of price breaks, and analyzing the stability of production-inventory control systems under shortages.

This early operations-research work laid the quantitative modeling foundation that carries through to Thenmozhi's current machine learning and forecasting projects.
